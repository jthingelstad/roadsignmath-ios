# A pass on a real phone

What to try when a TestFlight build lands, in about fifteen minutes. A
TestFlight build reads the live site, so everything here is real: the sign
you post is on roadsignmath.xyz a minute later. That's the point of this
pass, and why only a person runs it, never an agent.

Note anything that surprises you, with a screenshot, and the build
number (the TestFlight app shows it).

## 1. First look (signed out)

- [ ] First launch opens on sign-in. "Look around first" opens Home.
- [ ] Home lists the signs, newest first, with their math drawn like a
      book's. A long equation shrinks to fit rather than being cut off.
- [ ] A sign opens its page: photos, the math, who found it, where.
- [ ] Map: pins and clusters; tapping one opens its sign. Search finds a
      sign by town, by a finder's name and by one of its numbers.
- [ ] A link to a sign on roadsignmath.xyz (from Messages or Mail) opens
      the app on that sign.

## 2. Sign in

- [ ] Sign in with your email. The email arrives within a minute and
      looks right in your mail app, light and dark.
- [ ] Tapping the email's button opens the app and signs you in. (Or type
      the 6-digit code; try both on separate runs if you can.)
- [ ] The first time, the name step shows the address your name makes,
      and says it won't change.
- [ ] Home's avatar opens You: your name and email, sign out.

## 3. Find a sign (as the passenger)

Somewhere a sign is safe to photograph: a parked car, a walk, or a photo
already in your library.

- [ ] Capture opens on the camera, with the passenger label. It turns
      sideways with the phone. 1× matches the Camera app's 1×; 2× is
      there on a phone with a telephoto.
- [ ] The shutter freezes the shot and boxes each number as it's read.
- [ ] A photo from the library works too, and brings its own day and
      place.
- [ ] Check the numbers: each number has a close-up of where it was read.
      Untick one that isn't on the sign (a plate, an arrow read as 7).
      Tap one to fix it. Tap the photo where a number was missed.

## 4. Solve and post

- [ ] Solve it: tap tiles and the keypad to write the math. Tiles tick off
      as they're used. "It works" shows when it's true.
- [ ] Post it: the place (from the photo, or say where), who found it.
      Post. It's on Home, the Map and roadsignmath.xyz.
- [ ] "Post unsolved" from Check the numbers posts a sign with no math
      yet, for someone else to find.

## 5. Odds and ends

- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, post a sign, turn it off: it goes when the
      phone is back online.
- [ ] Larger Text (Settings > Accessibility) and dark mode both read well.
- [ ] Settings > App icon: pick one of the four others. The system says
      the icon changed, the tick moves, and the Home Screen shows it; it's
      still ticked after you close and reopen the app. Inside the app the
      bar still shows the green Highway sign. Long-press the Home Screen,
      Edit > Customize, and try Dark and Tinted: the icon keeps its shape
      in each. Pick Highway again to go back.
- [ ] VoiceOver reads the numbers, the tiles and "It works" sensibly.
- [ ] Delete my account (You) is there. Only try it with a spare account,
      such as a plus address: it removes every sign that account posted.
